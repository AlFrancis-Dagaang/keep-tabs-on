# Retro: T-01 to T-11
Date: 2026-11-14 (project ran 9–14 Nov 2026)

## What shipped
- In six days the app went from skeleton to live on Vercel: accounts, activity, bills, debts (both directions), goals, budget with history, Overview and first-run setup. CI runs on PRs and main.
- RLS isolation is tested against real Neon. Money is stored as integer centavos.

## What went well
- The validate loop ran on every task: lint, tsc, tests and build, plus scratch flow tests against Neon and the owner's click-through. Bugs surfaced in validate, not in use.
- Every task has a plan, an outcome and a dated follow-up list. Lessons were recorded after corrections (8 so far).
- Shortcut comments (`loadBalances` loading every entry, and others) kept early tasks small, each with an upgrade point.
- Decisions were written down: stack, RLS claims, owner-only, dev branch, migrate in the build.

## What went wrong
- JS Date dropped microseconds (T-04): a payment closing a bill cycle counted toward the next. Fixed; lesson written.
- Suspense around `usePathname` (T-05) broke the build and cost a mid-apply question. Lesson stayed "owed" through T-06 to T-08; lessons waited too long to be recorded.
- Option labels hid their contents (T-09): the owner expected PHP and USD only after picking "short fixed list". Lesson written.
- Net worth was built, then reversed (T-11): the formula was approved in words, not as a number. Lesson written.
- Production lagged dev: migrations 0003–0007 were first applied at deploy time. Fixed on 14 Nov by migrating in the Vercel build.
- Docs drifted: `arch.md` and `brief.md` were behind (budget limits, settings, start over); caught at T-07 and T-09, fixed 14 Nov.
- RLS tests are flaky (about 4 in 25 runs, first run after a pause). Cause unconfirmed, so no lesson.
