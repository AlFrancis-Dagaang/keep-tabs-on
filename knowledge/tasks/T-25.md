# T-25: Phone bottom bar and Activity opens on today

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Phone nav: below 768px the top bar, burger and drawer are gone. A bottom tab bar replaces them, matching `prototypes/mobile-bottom-bar.html` (approved): Overview, Accounts, Activity, Bills and More, using the same icons as the sidebar. The current page's tab is lit (Bills stays lit on `/bills/[id]`, and so on for the other tabs)
- [ ] More: opens a sheet with Installments, Goals, Debts, Budget, Set aside and Settings. Choosing a page closes it. More is lit on the pages it holds. Sign out and the theme toggle live on the Settings page below 768px (the sidebar keeps them from 768px up, which is unchanged)
- [ ] Phone fit: the bar stays at the bottom, page content is never hidden behind it, each tap target is at least 44px, and there is no sideways scroll at 390px
- [ ] Activity default: opening `/activity` with no `from`/`to` shows only today's entries (Asia/Manila date). The picker button reads "Today" then; an empty day says so
- [ ] Picker: the month grid, Today, This month and All dates. "Month to date" and "Custom range…" are gone, with their form and state. "All dates" still reaches everything, and a link with `from`/`to` in the URL (a month, or an older bookmark) still filters correctly
- [ ] Scope: the account, bill and installment pages differ only in that their picker loses Month to date and Custom range; Overview's Income and Expenses cards open Activity on this month and Recent activity's View all on all dates; no balance, query or schema change beyond the Activity default
- [ ] `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test` and `pnpm build` pass; `docs/design.md` describes the bottom bar and the new Activity default; looked at signed in on dev at 390px and desktop

## Findings
- Nav today: `components/mobile-nav.tsx` is a sticky top bar plus a left `Sheet` drawer; `components/app-sidebar.tsx` owns the `pages` list and `NavLinks` (Suspense around `usePathname`, per the 2026-11-13 lesson). `NavLinks`' `onNavigate` exists only for the drawer. `app/(app)/layout.tsx` is `flex-col md:flex-row` with `<MobileNav />` above `<main>`.
- Nothing else is fixed or sticky at the bottom, so a fixed bar at `z-30` sits under dialogs and sheets (`z-50`).
- The prototype has no `Covers: T-25` comment yet; apply adds one (its first line).
- **The picker is shared.** `MonthPicker` is used by `DateFilter`, and `DateFilter` is used by `/accounts/[id]`, `/bills/[id]` and `/installments/[id]`. Removing "Month to date" and "Custom range…" from `MonthPicker` removes them there too. That conflicts with the Scope criterion (see question 1).
- **Links into Activity now land on today.** `app/(app)/page.tsx` links "Income this month" and "Expenses this month" to `/activity?type=income|expense` and Recent activity "View all" to `/activity`. All three used to show every date; with the new default they would show only today (see question 2).
- Activity: `parseRange` runs before `today` is computed (`activity/page.tsx` lines 67 and 92); `typeHref` rebuilds `from`/`to` from the parsed range. `parseRange` returns `{}` for a bad or reversed range, which used to mean all dates.
- Tests: `parseRange` and `monthRange` are unit-tested in `lib/money.test.ts` and are not changed. No test touches the picker or the nav.
- Lessons applying: Suspense around `usePathname` (2026-11-13), mock placement first (2026-11-16, satisfied by the approved prototype), tick criteria when done (2026-11-13). No DB command, migration or schema change, so the DB-host and DB-test lessons do not apply.

## Left out
- A single-day pick in the picker (yesterday = pick the month), and any "Yesterday" shortcut.
- Changing what the Overview cards show or where they point beyond question 2.
- Activity columns per type, audit log, bill detail changes (all separate backlog items).
- A top bar or title on phones; the sidebar from 768px up; any change to `ThemeToggle`, `SignOutButton` or `Sheet` internals.
- Production: no migration, nothing touches the DB.

## Open questions (answered, kept for the record)
1. **The picker is shared, so the Scope criterion can't hold as written.** Removing "Month to date" and "Custom range…" from `MonthPicker` also removes them from the account, bill and installment pages (`DateFilter`). My recommendation: remove them everywhere, one picker with one behaviour, and change the Scope line to "those pages only lose the two rows". The alternative is a prop that keeps the two rows on the detail pages, which keeps dead code alive. Which?
2. **Overview links into Activity.** "Income this month", "Expenses this month" and Recent activity "View all" would now open on today only. My recommendation: point the two cards at the current month (`from`/`to` of this month) and "View all" at `?all=1`. This is three link edits in `app/(app)/page.tsx`, outside the stated criteria. Yes, or leave them and accept they show today?
3. **The More sheet covers the bar.** A modal sheet makes everything outside it inert, so the prototype's "sheet above a still-tappable bar" would need a non-modal custom panel. I plan the standard bottom sheet over the bar, closed by tapping outside or choosing a page. Is that okay?
4. Confirm: the Bills tab says "Bills" (the sidebar keeps "Recurring bills").
5. Confirm: no new test (see step 3).
