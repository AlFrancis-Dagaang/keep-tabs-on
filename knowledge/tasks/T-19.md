## Findings
- **The Overview is not "light" today.** `app/(app)/page.tsx` already has five stat cards (Net worth, Income, Expenses, Bills due in 7 days, Budget left), an Accounts tile grid, Upcoming bills, Goals, Debts and Recent activity. The "light Overview" in the old `design.md` was never built, so T-19 is a restyle toward the owner's image, not a cut-down. `design.md` (Overview page, Dates/icons, Shapes) was rewritten to the image and is uncommitted.
- **Most figures already exist.** `monthTotals(month, entries)`, `billsDueSoon`, `netWorth`, `progressBlocks`, `PixelBar`, `EntryDate` ("Today" tag) and `Section`/`MiniTable` are reused. `loadBalances` already returns every entry, so last month's income and expenses need no new query, only a previous-month helper (`nextMonth` exists in `lib/money.ts`, `previousMonth` doesn't).
- **Gaps against the image.**
  - Pixel bar and % change on the stat cards: new.
  - "Due soon / Upcoming" pills: today there's only "Overdue". Keep Overdue (it's a real state the image doesn't show).
  - Accounts as an icon list (today: tiles); Goals and Debts as lists with bar and % (today: bar and "x of y").
  - Three-column desktop layout (today: one column).
  - Budget left card: remove (owner confirmed the image wins).
- **Icons.** `accounts` has no icon column. Account names are shown on Accounts (`accounts/page.tsx`), the account detail page, Overview, and as plain text in account selects in the forms (entry, bill, installment, debt, goal, setup). `app/auth/sign-in/page.tsx` is the only place `next/image` is used.
- **Lessons that apply.** 2026-11-16 mock-placement-first (mock before code; the criteria already say so), 2026-11-14 show-figure-on-real-data (the % change and card bars are new headline figures; judge them on real numbers before building), 2026-11-13 tick-criteria-when-done, 2026-11-14 check-db-host (before `db:migrate` or tests).
- `prototypes/` has `mobile.html` (T-16) only; nothing covers T-19. No code graph needed.

My default: each card's share of the largest of the four. It's decoration, not a real measure (net worth against bills means nothing). The honest alternatives are Income and Expenses bars showing "spent so far as a share of income", and Net worth and Bills with no bar. Pick one; the mock will show it.
