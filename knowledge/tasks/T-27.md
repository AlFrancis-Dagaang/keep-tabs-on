# T-27: Insights, where the money goes

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Insights page: a sidebar item and a tab in the phone More sheet, matching `prototypes/insights.html` (approved). A month picker with previous and next arrows (`?month=YYYY-MM`, default this month in Asia/Manila, never after it), then Income, Spent and Left for that month
- [ ] Where it went: the month's expenses grouped by category, biggest first, each with its amount, its share of spending and the change against the month before (hidden when that month has none). Expenses with no category are grouped as Bills, Debts, Installments (their payments) or Uncategorized. Bars use the existing look; no chart library
- [ ] Last 6 months: income and spent bars side by side for the six months ending at the chosen one, the chosen month marked
- [ ] A written line built from the numbers (a template, no AI, no API): spent, change against last month, the top category's share, what is left of income. An empty month says so
- [ ] Money rules: only income and expense entries count; transfers (between accounts, to goals, to someone else) are left out; nothing is stored, no schema change; the figures come from the same entries Activity shows, so they match it
- [ ] Phone fit: every tap target at least 44px and no sideways scroll at 390px
- [ ] `docs/brief.md` no longer lists charts and reports as non-goals ("AI chat" stays); `docs/design.md` describes the page; `pnpm lint`, `pnpm test` and `pnpm build` pass; looked at signed in on dev at 390px and desktop

## Findings
- Reusable today: `monthTotals`, `previousMonth`, `monthChange` and `monthRange` in `lib/money.ts` (income and expenses per month, % change, the date range of a month), `getMoney` for formatting, `PageHeader`, the `Budget` page's `monthName`, `loadBalances` is not needed. `MonthPicker` is range based (Activity's filter), so the mock's simple previous/next arrows are new, as links with `?month=`.
- Nothing is stored: every figure is a sum over `transactions` where `type` is `income` or `expense`. Bill, debt and installment payments are expenses with no category (`schema.ts`), so without a rule they would all land in one nameless bucket; the criteria name them.
- `categories.kind` is `spending` or `income`; a category deleted later leaves `category_id` null on its entries, which also becomes Uncategorized.
- A deleted bill, debt or installment sets its link to null, so its old payments fall into Uncategorized too. Accepted, not worth a snapshot.
- Scale: like Budget (`shortcut:` comment there), load the last 6 months of entries per request and sum in a pure function; switch to SQL sums if a user passes a few thousand rows a month.
- The brief's non-goals line "AI chat, charts beyond progress bars, reports" conflicts with this task; the owner chose numbers only, so "charts beyond progress bars, reports" goes and "AI chat" stays.
- Lessons applying: mock first (satisfied by `prototypes/insights.html`), tick criteria when done, Suspense around `usePathname` (the new nav item uses the existing `pages` list, no new `usePathname` use), show the figure on real data (look at it on dev data, not only the mock). No schema or migration, so DB-host and DB-test lessons do not apply except that the new test is pure and needs no DB.

## Left out
- Anything with the Claude API or any AI; the written line is a template.
- A "saved" figure, savings goals in the breakdown, budgets or limits on this page (Budget owns them).
- Per-account or per-payee breakdowns, custom date ranges, export, a donut or any chart other than the 6-month bars.
- A desktop or phone shortcut to Insights from Overview.
